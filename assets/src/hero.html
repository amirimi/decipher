Govern every model call

Goal

Brownout's requirements hold for every call a governed team makes, and its downgrade reaches every text-generation endpoint. Today both stop at /v1/chat/completions. A team whose clients call /v1/messages, such as Claude Code, or /v1/responses, such as Codex, is never downgraded. A model added unpriced after init is refused for chat but served through those endpoints, where LiteLLM records $0. This replaces specs/non-chat-calls.md, which kept the chat-only scope, and closes non-chat-pass-through.

Sequence

The pre-call hook sorts each call by its call_type into one kind, in one table. It then checks the team and the key on every call, checks the model on every call that names one, and runs the tier decision on every call that names a model. Only a text-generation call can be downgraded. It writes its outcome into the brownout field. The router calls the filter hook, which verifies every call that names a model against that outcome and passes a call without a model. The headers hook discloses the outcome on every response to a call that names a model.
